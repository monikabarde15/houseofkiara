A band can be switched off with 
“Show this band on the page”; it 
then stays on the paper, faded and 
marked hidden. The picture is 
uploaded to the media store; the 
page keeps its name, alt text and 
crop. Darkening is offered only for 
the full bleed layout, and below 25% 
the panel warns that the headline is 
hard to read. The brand film slot 
says the film is coming rather than 
showing an empty player.
