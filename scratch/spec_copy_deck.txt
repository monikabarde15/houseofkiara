Only what you have agreed to, or what the law requires: one of the costs that can be yours [opens answer e-costs],

for a sale reversed because something was not disclosed. 
Search keywords: deduct deduction taken from payout less reduce owe 
04  Tax and your PAN   italic: your PAN  ·  bar label: Tax  ·  id: s-tax 
GST, tax deducted from payouts, and the tax on your own earnings. 
e-gst   Is GST taken from my share? 
Tag: Never from your share  (good news, sage) 
No. GST is paid by the renter or buyer on top of the price, and we pay it to the government. Your share is worked 
out on the price before GST. 
Search keywords: gst my share tax renter buyer pays government 
e-pan   Why do you ask for my PAN? 
We ask for your PAN before your piece goes live, together with your payout details, because the law can require 
us to deduct tax from some payouts. When it does, we deduct it and send you the certificate, so you can claim it in 
your income tax return. 
We never ask for your full Aadhaar number. 
Search keywords: pan tds tax deducted source certificate aadhaar income tax

e-owntax   Do I pay tax on my earnings? 
You are responsible for any tax on your earnings, and your payout statements give you a clear record for your

05  Prices and offers   italic: offers  ·  bar label: Prices and offers  ·  id: s-prices 
How the price of your piece is set, and how offers work. 
e-price   Who sets the price of my piece? 
Tag: Agreed with you  (good news, sage) 
We agree it with you. We suggest a rental price, a daily rate and a sale price from your piece's designer, condition 
and demand, and nothing goes live until you are happy with them. 
We never lower a price on our own, and there is no fixed listing period and no automatic markdown. A price 
changes only when you agree. 
Search keywords: price set who decides my piece value rental price daily rate sale price markdown 
e-offers   How do offers on my piece work? 
Tag: Preloved only  (neutral, gold deep) 
Offers are taken only on pieces for sale, never on rentals. We agree with you the lowest offer we can accept, and 
only ever accept offers at or above it. Your share is then calculated on the price the buyer pays. 
Search keywords: offer offers negotiate lowest minimum accept make an offer
