08 Three Ways 
Rent, Buy Preloved and 
List & Sell, and a line 
about the fourth way. 
On the page 
• The words: Eyebrow · Heading · Line under the heading 
• The three doors: Numeral · Title · Lead line · Body 
• The line underneath: Line 
Page settings 
• Showing the band: the switch “Show this band on the 
page” 
• The three doors: Button, on each of 3 rows 
• The line underneath: the switch “Show it” 
The three doors are the platform’s 
ways in; a fourth would promise a 
way the catalogue cannot serve, so 
none is added here. Each door’s 
button is a setting, and the line 
about the fourth way can be 
switched off.
