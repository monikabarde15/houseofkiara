Overview and scope 
The Contact Us page is where a customer reaches the House of Kaira team in the way that suits them. Every way 
of reaching us comes first, then the six pages that answer most questions, then a short note form, a visit by 
appointment, and the formal details a support page must carry. 
Property Value 
Page Contact Us 
Browser title Contact Us · House of Kaira 
Meta description Reach House of Kaira on WhatsApp, by email or by phone, visit us in Indore by 
appointment, and find our Grievance Officer and business details. 
Breadcrumb Home / Contact Us 
Primary action Message us on WhatsApp 
Bands, in order 1  Hero: introduction, live status and the Ways to reach us card 
2  Answers, any time: the six pages that answer most questions 
3  Write us a note, with Visit us in Indore and the arrival note beside it 
4  Raising a concern, and Our details 
Shared site components The announcement bar, header, navigation drawer and footer are the site’s shared 
components and are out of scope. They appear on this page unchanged. Below 1200px 
the header is the phone style header with the menu button, as on every page. The footer 
is the light beige footer (#F5EFE6) used across the site; its Support column marks 
Contact Us as the current page (charcoal, weight 500). 
Content source Every figure is a Site Token (Section 04). Every line of copy is editable in the admin 
Pages module, Contact Us entry. 
Layout width Content is centred with a maximum width of 1180px. Side gutters: 48px at 1200px and 
wider, 32px from 901 to 1199px, 28px from 761 to 900px, 18px at 760px and below. 
Breakpoints 1200px and wider  ·  901 to 1199px  ·  761 to 900px  ·  760px and below  ·  380px and 
below (Section 12) 
Screenshot pack Shared on drive.

How the status is worked out 
• Take the current day and time in Asia/Kolkata. 
• Open when today is a support day and the time is at or after open and before close. 
• Before opening on a support day: closed title without a day (“We’re back at 10 AM IST”). 
• Otherwise find the next support day: “tomorrow” when it is the next day, else “on” and the day’s name. The 
time is the opening time written as 10 AM. 
• Work it out again every 60 seconds, and only update the text when the state changes.

13.1  Structure 
• A skip link, “Skip to contact details”, is the first thing a keyboard reaches on the page. It stays out of sight until 
it has focus, then shows at the top left (position: absolute; left: 16px; top: -64px; z-index: 1000; background: 
var(--charcoal); color: var(--cream); padding: 10px 14px; font-size: 13px; text-decoration: none; with focus: 
top: 12px) and jumps to the page content. 
• The page content is one main element (id cu-main, the skip link’s target). Bands 1 to 3 are sections named by 
their headings; Band 4 is named “Raising a concern, and our details”. The side column is an aside named 
“Visits, and when a piece arrives”. The breadcrumb is a nav named Breadcrumb. 
• One h1. Headings in order: h1 We’d love to hear from you; h2 Ways to reach us; h3 WhatsApp; h3 Email; h3 
Call; h2 Answers, any time; h2 Write us a note; h2 Visit us in Indore; h3 Has a piece just arrived, and 
something isn’t right?; h2 Raising a concern; h2 Our details; h3 Sending something to us?. 
13.2  Form 
• The form is named “Write us a note” and switches off the browser’s own validation bubbles (novalidate), so 
only the page’s messages show. 
• Every field has a visible label tied to it. Required fields carry required and aria-required; the asterisk itself is 
hidden from screen readers. 
• Each field points to its hint and its error message with aria-describedby, and sets aria-invalid while it needs 
fixing. 
• Autocomplete: name, email, tel. The WhatsApp field opens the phone keypad (inputmode tel); spellcheck is 
off on Email. 
• Focus: on a failed check, to the first field to fix; on success, to the thank you message; after Write another 
note, to Your name. 
13.3  Everything else 
• Every control is a real link, button, input or select, reachable by keyboard, with the gold focus ring. 
• Links that open WhatsApp open a new tab, with rel noopener. 
• Icons are decorative and hidden from screen readers; the Copy button is named “Copy email address”. 
• Reduced motion switches off every transition and animation.
